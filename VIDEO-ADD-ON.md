# Video & Motion — the add-on

> **Not installed by default. On purpose.**

Most people want a Partner AI to run their week — briefs, memory, admin, client work.
Video is a different job, and bundling it made everyone's install longer to serve the
few who wanted it. So [AI_NAME] asks once, and only installs it if you say yes.

---

## The button

Say any of these to [AI_NAME], now or in six months:

> *"I need the video add-on, go ahead."*
> *"Add video."* · *"Set up the video tools."*

[AI_NAME] then fetches and runs the add-on's own install playbook:

```
Install the Video & Motion add-on from
https://github.com/DanJoachimn/Partner-Ai-Kit-Video —
read INSTALL.md and walk me through it.
```

~15 minutes. It asks before each piece; skipping is a normal answer.

---

## Should you say yes?

**Yes if** you publish video, record Looms or course modules, want animated graphics
generated from code, or want long videos transcribed instead of watched.

**No if** you mostly write, brief and run admin. The main kit already covers you, and
you can add this the first time you think *"I wish [AI_NAME] could just cut this."*

Nothing in the main kit depends on it. Saying no costs you nothing later.

---

## What's in it

- **Watch** — any video (YouTube, Loom, Zoom, local file) becomes a transcript and a summary
- **Edit** — cut, trim and assemble real footage; find music, sound effects and voiceover
- **Animate** — motion graphics rendered from code, no timeline dragging
- **Generate** — images and clips via 1200+ AI models
- **Four written guides**, including the pre-production rules — the decisions to lock
  *before* you record, because most of them can't be fixed afterwards

**Cost:** the skills are free. A couple of the tools they drive bill per use (image
generation, premium voices) — both optional, both with free tiers, and the installer
shows you the free path first.
